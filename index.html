Finale Chapter Guide

These rules apply only during WL2 Finale Chapter. Please read thru every section below and good luck!

Late Rules & Penalties

2 mincounts as late （遲到）
5 mincounts as missing （鴿班）

Rules for leaving in the middle of an hour or taking someone's spot without asking, are the same as the regular rules.

⚠️
Notice

Your runner or filler role will be removed if you accumulate up to 3 lates or 2 missings.

How to Get a Runner / Filler Role

Send a screenshot of your tier/fill team to #審核區, and specify which role you're applying for.

Filler: a 3.2× multiplier / 220% ISV minimum fill team is required.

Runner: see the requirements below.

💡
Reminder

If a filler does not have the runner role but brings their own tier team into a room, other people in the room are allowed to ask them to leave.

If you are a runner and need an emergency ping role (a.k.a. 補班), go to #補位身分自助區 to get the filler role. The runner role cannot be pinged during the Finale Chapter — ping the 補班 role instead if you need someone to cover, snipe, or fill.

Runner Requirements

  • Your oshi's WL2 leader card at skill level 4 is required.
  • All runners must change their server nickname to include their multiplier and talent.

Example: 初音 R3.09 36w

How to Host a Room

There are two types of rooms: scheduled rooms (固車) and general rooms (date-prefixed).

Scheduled rooms: if you already have someone (or a group) who will run with you at specific times, you can host a room based on the hours you've all scheduled, and find runners or fillers willing to follow the same schedule.

General rooms: same etiquette and format as regular wl rules.

The emergency channel is open for both scheduled and general room hosts.

General room channels will be deleted by Mana after each day ends, so you don't need to delete your own messages.
